Instagram Image Sizes

Use the correct canvas before posting so Instagram does not crop important words, logos, dates, or faces.

Feed post1080 × 1350 px

Portrait, 4:5. Recommended for most flyers and announcements because it uses more screen space.

Square post1080 × 1080 px

Square, 1:1. Use only when the design was created specifically for a square canvas.

Story or Reel1080 × 1920 px

Full-screen vertical, 9:16. Keep critical text and logos away from the top and bottom interface areas.

Before posting

  • Do not stretch a landscape Facebook image into an Instagram portrait frame.
  • Do not crop a flyer without checking whether dates, addresses, sponsor logos, or calls to action were removed.
  • For a carousel, use the same orientation for every slide.
  • Export at 1080 pixels wide for feed images.
  • Review the final preview inside Instagram before publishing.
Instagram supports feed-photo aspect ratios from 1.91:1 through 3:4 and preserves images up to 1080 pixels wide. Reels support up to a 9:16 vertical frame and should be at least 720 pixels in resolution.
